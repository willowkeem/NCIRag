Details of current Postgraduate Fees for full-time and part-time students in NCI.
Postgraduate students studying in NCI
Full-Time
Students for full-time postgraduate courses may be eligible for an SUSI grant. To check whether you qualify for a grant visit
www.studentfinance.ie
.
Students that are not in receipt of an SUSI grant can pay the full amount of fees before course commencement or you can pay half of the fees before the course starts and the balance in January.
Alternatively NCI have a direct debit plan that allows students to pay 20% of the fees upfront and the remainder in equal instalments.
