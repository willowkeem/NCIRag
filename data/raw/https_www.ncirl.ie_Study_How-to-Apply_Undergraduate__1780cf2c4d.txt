Foundation Level Mathematics
A pass in Foundation Level Mathematics at B2 or higher will be considered as meeting the minimum entry requirements for the college's
Higher Certificate in Business
. When determining Leaving Certificate points, foundation maths can be applied. The scale of points is as follows:
F1 - 20
F2 - 12

Deferred Entry
A student who has been offered a place may defer acceptance for one year under certain circumstances which are deemed appropriate by the College. Applications for deferrals will not be considered after the end of the first semester except in extenuating circumstances. In relation to deferred entry into full-time day programmes, the College operates a policy in common with other third level institutions. For details see the CAO handbook or
www.cao.ie
