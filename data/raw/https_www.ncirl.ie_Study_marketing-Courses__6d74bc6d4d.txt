Studying Marketing at NCI
Gaining a qualification in marketing can be your passport to a career in a variety of areas, whether you’re interested in digital marketing, brand management, market research, sales or product development.
National College of Ireland's marketing programmes are designed to give graduates the skills they need to succeed in the modern marketing world. Graduating with a degree in marketing means following a career path that is futureproofed, exciting, challenging and dynamic.
