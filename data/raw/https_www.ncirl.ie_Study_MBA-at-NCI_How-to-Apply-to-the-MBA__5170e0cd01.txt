How to Apply to the MBA
Details on how to apply for the MBA course at NCI
Application Process
Your application for the MBA at NCI should take no more than 15 minutes. In just a few simple steps you can reserve your place for the next cohort of MBA students. Here are a few things to bear in mind:
Entry Requirements for the MBA
Applicants are required to hold a minimum of a 2.2 honours degree in either a cognate or non-cognate area at Level 8 on the National Framework of Qualifications and a minimum of three years’ work experience, ideally but not necessarily, in management. The college operates a
Recognition of Prior Experiential Learning (RPEL)
scheme, meaning that non-graduates with extensive professional experience and other prior qualifications may also be considered for the course.
Required documents:
Current CV
Final degree transcripts from your previous college/university
Steps involved in the application process:
Start by visiting
the MBA course page
Click the Apply Now button
Fill in your personal details
Add your employment information
Complete your education history
Upload your documentation
Review your application
