MBA Student Profile
The MBA students in NCI come from a diverse set of backgrounds, making the course community a rich, varied and interesting one.
MBA Students
Our MBA students come from a wide and diverse range of industries, yet have one thing in common – the desire to work hard and progress quickly in their career, whether that’s moving into senior management or perhaps taking the plunge and starting a business of their own.
Nationality
The MBA attracts a diverse profile of student from all sectors as well as nationalities. Approximately 85 per cent of the students on our MBA course have come from Ireland. The remaining 15 per cent have come from countries such as Poland, Italy, France, Pakistan, India and Russia.
