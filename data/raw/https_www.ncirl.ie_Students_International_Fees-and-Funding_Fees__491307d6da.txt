Fees for International Students
Tuition fees cover the cost of your study and include charges for registration, tuition, supervision and examinations.
Fees information for International students can be found on this page.
Payment of fees will also entitle you to membership of the library, the Students' Union and the on-campus gym. Additional fees are required where you have to retake modules, resit exams or repeat part or all of a year.
To provide you with certainty in your financial planning, we have fixed tuition fees for new international students joining multi-year programmes.
Any offer of a place at NCI is made on the understanding that you have sufficient funds to pay for your tuition fees and your living expenses for the entire duration of your period of study.
