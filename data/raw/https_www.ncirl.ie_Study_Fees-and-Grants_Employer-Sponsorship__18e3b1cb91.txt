Employer Sponsorship
If your fees are being paid by your company you will need to complete a
sponsorship form
and return it to the Fees Office.  In order to issue an invoice to your employer the form needs to be completed and returned to the Fees Office.  This can be posted to:
Fees Office
National College of Ireland
Mayor Street
IFSC
Dublin 1
Or you can email it to
fees@ncirl.ie
.
When the form has been received, the company will be issued an invoice within five working days.
