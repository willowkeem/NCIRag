Courses in Psychology
Current Psychology Courses at NCI
NCI offers a BA Honours in Psychology, a degree accredited by the
Psychological Society of Ireland (PSI)
.
On completion of this course you will receive a Quality and Qualifications Ireland (QQI) degree at level 8 on the National Framework of Qualifications.
The degree programme is available as both a
full-time course
and a
part-time course
.
BA (Honours) in Psychology - Full-Time
3 Years | Level 8 | Campus
This programme is available to students who are applying through the Central Applications Office (CAO) process and also to mature students over 23 years of age who wish to study full-time.
Course Details
BA (Honours) in Psychology - Part-Time
4 Years | Level 8 | Blended
Running two evenings a week and some Saturdays, this course will appeal to more mature students for whom evening study is preferable. You do not need to have previously studied psychology.
