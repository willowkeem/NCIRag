English Language Requirements
Students whose first language is not English are required to have an appropriate score in an approved examination in English language before they can register on a programme at NCI.

Disability Access Route to Education
The Disability Access Route to Education (DARE) is a supplementary admissions scheme for school leavers with disabilities.
Higher Education Access Route
The Higher Education Access Route (HEAR) is a third level admissions scheme for school leavers from socio-economically disadvantaged backgrounds.
Recognition of Prior Experiential Learning
Recognition of Prior Experiential Learning (RPEL) is a scheme which grants formal recognition of knowledge or experience that has been gained from work, life or previous study.
