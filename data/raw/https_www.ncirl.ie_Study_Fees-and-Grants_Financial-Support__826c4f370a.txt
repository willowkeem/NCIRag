Financial Support
A number of financial supports are available for students who need help with college expenses while studying at NCI.
Grants & Funding
Information on grants that may be available to students studying in NCI, along with links to some useful websites regarding financial support.
Student Assistance Fund
The Student Assistance Fund provides financial support for disadvantaged students and emergency financial assistance for other students.
Fund for Students with Disabilities
The Fund for Students with Disabilities allocates funding for services and supports to full-time and part-time students with disabilities.
Laptop Loan Scheme
This government grant allows NCI to provide eligible students with a laptop loan to assist them in completing their chosen course.
Scholarships & Discounts
NCI has scholarships for exceptional students both from NCI and other colleges. We also have discount offers for NCI alumni.
