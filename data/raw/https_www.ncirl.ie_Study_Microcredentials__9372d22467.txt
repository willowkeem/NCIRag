Microcredentials at NCI
Microcredentials
are short, sharp, programmes that give you an in-depth understanding of a key skill area in a focused way.
Microcredentials are accredited postgraduate programmes delivered online with some classroom seminar options, by experienced faculty. 
Programmes cover the latest thinking in business, computing, and education. They are assessed using continuous assessment and final exams.
Microcredentials are drawn from our postgraduate programmes so should you decide to progress to a full award you will get credit for this module. 
If you need to get quickly up to speed in these emerging areas these programmes are for you.
