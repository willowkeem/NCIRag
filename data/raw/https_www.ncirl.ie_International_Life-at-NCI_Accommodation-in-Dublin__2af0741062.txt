One of the biggest decisions you will have to make is where you would like to live in Dublin.
We highly recommend booking Student Residences and to apply early and to multiple locations
Information for students considering living in student residence accommodation
Student Residence accommodation is purpose built student accommodation and is the only long-term accommodation you can book in advance of your arrival to Dublin. This saves you valuable time, as you don't need to search for accommodation when you arrive.
Most of the residential options are fairly new and purpose-built properties all around Dublin city. Some options are within walking distance of NCI, others are well connected by public transport.
What to consider
Pros
Advance booking from your home country
Time-saving as no need for accommodation search in Dublin
Secure living
New, purpose-built & stylish properties with great facilities
Sociable environment
On-site maintenance
Cons
You may need to book for the academic year
Can sell out quickly
Can be expensive
House rules may not suit your lifestyle
You do not get to choose who you live with
Difficult to cancel contract you have committed for
