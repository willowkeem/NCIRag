RPEL Applications
To apply for a course through RPEL you will need to submit a programme application via the
How to Apply
link in addition to submitting the relevant RPEL application form, which can be found below:
RPEL for Entry Form
RPEL for Exemption Form
If you have already applied for a course and have been directed to fill out a form please click on the appropriate link above and submit this documentation by post or send to
admissions@ncirl.ie
quoting your name, address and course you have applied for.
