Postgraduate Programmes in Educational Practice at NCI
Move to the forefront of learning insight and innovation by completing the Master of Arts in Educational Practice.
These are exciting times in learning and education with developments in areas such as pedagogic design, technology enhanced learning, new approaches to skills and new understanding of what it takes for learning success.  

Our Level Nine programmes are for professionals who wish to become expert in their chosen area.
The
Master of Arts in Educational Practice
culminates with the completion of a scholarly dissertation on a specialist topic. We encourage practitioner investigations and facilitate participants to make meaningful contributions to the real world challenges of learning and teaching.
The masters programme is stimulating and challenging and intended for those with some experience in the field. We are mindful that the skills and methods of research and scholarship are often daunting at first. Our faculty will guide and empower you on this transformative learning journey.
The Centre delivers the following postgraduate programmes:
Masters of Arts in Educational Practice
(2 Year programme).
Postgraduate Diploma in Arts in Educational Practice in Teaching for Further Education
(1 Year Programme). This programme is accredited by the Teaching Council of Ireland for Further Education teachers.
Classes are delivered on Tuesday and/or Thursday evenings with some Saturday morning classes in the academic year.
