Academic entry requirements
All international students need to meet both our academic entry requirements and our English language requirements.
To learn about the academic entry requirements for your country, please visit our
country pages
and select the country where you were educated.
Please also look at the requirements in your chosen course. Certain courses will have more detailed requirements regarding what you will have needed to study previously. If you need any assistance on understanding our entry requirements or whether you are suitable for a particular course, please feel free to contact us or we are happy to introduce you to one of our approved agents.
