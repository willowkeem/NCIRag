MBA Course Content

MBA Course Content

The NCI MBA programme will provide you with a strong theoretical foundation in management as well as the practical application of theory in real world settings and cases.

MBA Programme

The MBA programme emphasises the development of effective business and management skills to solve management problems through structured analysis, problem solving techniques, effective decision-making, strong communication skills and leadership.

MBA students will be exposed to the latest thinking in the core disciplines of management, people and strategy, management of financial resources and economics for management. Our faculty will guide you through this thinking and help you apply it to real world scenarios. 

Study what YOU want

You will be able to choose from a wide range of electives in marketing and international business, human resource management, and entrepreneurship and information technology. You will have the opportunity to participate in an exciting management simulation game in your final semester, which takes a real-life business scenario from start to market, utilising the latest technology to allow you to apply all you have learned in a dynamic virtual business environment.

Get a global perspective

In a global business world our programme develops an international perspective with optional field trips available as part of the programme*. This affords you the opportunity to further develop the skills and cultural competencies to compete successfully in the world market. 

*The International field trip incurs an additional fee and is subject to student numbers.

Hone your practical & entrepreneurial skills

All programmes at National College of Ireland have a practical focus and an entrepreneurial perspective. The knowledge and skills acquired through our MBA will allow you to progress your own management career as well as to consider creating an enterprise of your own.

Many MBA programmes are not taught in a way that encourages students to think critically. The NCI programme emphasises experimentation, critical thinking, innovation and entrepreneurship. It will equip you for a successful career in the new dynamic world of management and also for enterprise start-up.
Dr Colette Darcy, Dean School of Business NCI

MBA Programme Schedule

Monday and Wednesday 18.00 - 21.00 plus some Saturday workshops.

Please note that exams can be scheduled during the morning, afternoon or evening Monday to Saturday.

Year 1 - Semester 1 - Sept-Dec

  • Enterprise Management
  • Economics for Management

Year 1 - Semester 2 - Feb-May

  • Managing Financial Resources
  • Strategic Marketing Management
  • One elective course from list

Year 2 - Semester 1 - Sept-Dec

  • Strategic Management and Global Environment
  • Corporate Governance, Business Ethics and CSR
  • Research Methods

Year 2 - Semester 2 - Jan-July

  • Effective Leadership in the Organisation
  • Managing People
  • One elective course from list

Year 2 - Semester 3 - July-Sept

Your final semester will be largely independent learning. It will consist of our Enterprise Simulation Game for 10 credits and you will complete a 20 credit dissertation.

Elective Course List

Please note that choices are subject to numbers

  • International Field Trip*
  • Services Marketing
  • Commercial Law
  • Strategic HRM
  • International Marketing
  • International Finance
  • e-Business Infrastructure
  • Managing e-Business Implementation
  • Operations Management

*The International field trip incurs an additional fee and is subject to student numbers. Visa restrictions may apply.

Find out more about all the modules covered on the course.

Award

The MBA programme is awarded by QQI at Level 9 on the National Framework of Qualifications (NFQ). This award will allow transfer to Level 9 research degrees and in some cases, progression to taught doctoral programmes at Level 10 on the NFQ.
