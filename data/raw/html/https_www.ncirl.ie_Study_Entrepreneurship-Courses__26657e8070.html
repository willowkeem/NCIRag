Find a course

Studying Entrepreneurship at NCI

If you have ever thought about creating your own enterprise, or if you want to bring more creativity and innovation to an existing organisation, then National College of Ireland can give you the skills and understanding you need.

Our range of Entrepreneurship courses give learners a critical insight into entrepreneurship through a deep exploration of the stages of creativity, innovation, and enterprise. Our courses encourage those with an entrepreneurial mindset to explore new innovations and provide them with the tools and practical frameworks and resources needed to explore new businesses and create new opportunities.

Visit the course pages to learn how to apply.

 

 

Master of Business Administration (MBA)

The MBA course will provide you with a strong theoretical foundation in the broader disciplines of management as well as focusing on the practical application of theory in real-world settings and cases.

Master of Business Administration (MBA)

The knowledge and skills acquired through our MBA will allow you to progress your own management career as well as encourage you to consider creating a business of your own.

2 Years | Level 9 | Part-Time | Blended

Master's Degrees in Entrepreneurship

The MSc in Entrepreneurship is offered both full-time and part-time. Online applications are now open, visit a course page to get started.

MSc in Entrepreneurship - Part-Time

The MSc in Entrepreneurship will provide you with a critical insight into entrepreneurship through a deep exploration of the stages of creativity, innovation and enterprise.

2 Years | Level 9 | Part-Time | Blended

MSc in Entrepreneurship - Full-Time

This course is aimed at individuals seeking to develop an entrepreneurial mindset with a view to starting their own business or nurturing this approach within an existing organisational structure.

Undergraduate Degrees in Entrepreneurship

NCI offers a number of undergraduate courses which help students to develop their entrepreneurial skills, including the BA (Honours) in Business and BA (Honours) in Marketing Practice.

BA (Honours) in Business

As part of this programme, students will evaluate the issues and processes involved in the successful creation of a new enterprise and develop an appreciation of the entrepreneurial mindset.

3 Years | Level 8 | Full-Time

See Course Details
BA (Honours) in Marketing Practice

As part of this programme, students are given the opportunity to showcase their entrepreneurial skillset through an exciting capstone project which involves developing an online business and presenting this brand to members of the NCI community.

3 Years | Level 8 | Full-Time
