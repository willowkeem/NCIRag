Applying to NCI

Our application process at NCI is very simple - but first you need to identify what type of course you would like to study. Below are some of the options available to you.

Scholarships & Discounts
NCI have scholarships for exceptional students both from NCI and other colleges. We also have discount offers for NCI alumni.
Find Out More
Advanced Entry
Students who hold a level 6 FETAC/QQI award may be eligible for advanced entry to certain courses in NCI.
More Information
English Language Requirements
Students whose first language is not English are required to have an appropriate score in an approved examination in English language before they can register on a programme at NCI.
Learn More

Disability Access Route to Education The Disability Access Route to Education (DARE) is a supplementary admissions scheme for school leavers with disabilities.
Higher Education Access Route The Higher Education Access Route (HEAR) is a third level admissions scheme for school leavers from socio-economically disadvantaged backgrounds.
Recognition of Prior Experiential Learning Recognition of Prior Experiential Learning (RPEL) is a scheme which grants formal recognition of knowledge or experience that has been gained from work, life or previous study.
