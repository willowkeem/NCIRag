International Guides

Information and resources for international students

Our award winning support services are world-class, including our International Office which is dedicated to the needs of our international students, ensuring you have all the information you need to arrive, live and study at NCI.


Your Journey to NCI
The Preparation Guide contains useful information and practical tips to help students plan for a successful, safe and stress-free journey to Ireland.
Download Preparation Guide

Support Hub Articles

We have compiled a library of guides and articles to provide you with answers to the most frequently asked questions. If you can't find the answer to your question, you can search all the articles in the NCI Support Hub.

Starting at NCI Starting at NCI, articles provide information for incoming students before arriving in Ireland and starting at NCI, such as how to find accommodation in Dublin, budgeting, and more.
Getting Established in Ireland Getting Established in Ireland support hub articles help students who have recently arrived in the country with essential information on how to request letters, apply for a PPS number, and more.
Academics Academic Support Hub articles help students throughout their academic journey at NCI by providing information on repeating exams, exiting with a postgraduate award, and more.

Visas & Immigration The Visa and Immigration Support Hub article provides key information on how to register with immigration, renew a student visa, and more.
Fees & Funding The Fees & Funding Support Hub articles provide information on how to request a statement of account, fees receipt, and more.
