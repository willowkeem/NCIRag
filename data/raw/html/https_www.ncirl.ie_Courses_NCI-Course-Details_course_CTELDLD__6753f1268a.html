NCI Course Details

Certificate in Technology Enhanced Learning and Digital Learning Design

Course Instance ID: 922946111
Description: Certificate in Technology Enhanced Learning and Digital Learning Design
Duration: 12 weeks
NFQ Level: Level 9

This course is designed to support existing learning professionals and pre-service teachers to develop the relevant knowledge, skills and competence for designing and facilitating technology-enhanced learning within relevant FET, ETB, adult and corporate learning sectors.

Course details

About this Course

Over the course of 12 weeks, you will:

  1. Compare and contrast the different approaches to the way in which technology can be used to enhance learning.
  2. Analyse and contrast the affordances for learning and the possible constraints in various e-learning, virtual, mobile learning and blended learning settings.
  3. Assess the quality of a learning environment using appropriate measures and criteria.
  4. Use pedagogic design to create effective, inclusive artefacts with the appropriate use of learning technologies.
  5. Critically evaluate standards and good practices in technology-based learning environments.
Who is the course for?

The programme provides a contextualised curriculum that meets the professional development needs of practitioners working across a variety of roles and contexts within the sector (such as an FET teacher, Learning development, Learning Designer, Learning Architect). It is an advanced level programme targeted at the practitioner who already has some basic experience of using simple digital technologies (such as Word, PowerPoint and internet applications). The programme will provide opportunities for the practitioner to critically reflect on and expand their digital practices in an inclusive, supportive learning environment.

Course Delivery

Blended - Livestream with some directed e-learning activities.

Award and Progression

The Certificate in Learning and Technology will lead to a special purpose award at Level 9 (10 ECTS credits) on the National Framework of Qualifications. Learners who successfully complete this course may opt to apply for the MA in Educational Practice.

Entry Requirements

Applicants holding an Honours Degree 2.2 (Level 8) in any discipline ideally with professional involvement in the facilitation of learning or training will be considered for the course. For applicants whose first language is not English, please note the English language entry requirements.

The college operates a Recognition of Prior Experiential Learning (RPEL) scheme, which means that applicants who do not meet the normal academic requirements may be considered based on relevant work or other relevant experience.

Applicants may be interviewed by the Programme Director or a member of the programme team.

Laptop Requirements

National College of Ireland has implemented a Bring Your Own Device policy for all courses. Specifically, students are expected to successfully participate in lectures, laboratories and projects using a portable computer (laptop/notebook) with a substantial hardware configuration.

The recommended configuration is as follows:

Processor:

  • Intel 11th Gen or newer, Core i3 / i5 /i7 or Ultra 3 / 5 /7
    Or
  • AMD 5000 Series or newer, Rzyen 3 / 5 /7

Random Access Memory (RAM):
16GB or greater

Storage:
SSD (Solid State Drive) 256GB or greater

Wireless Card:
802.11 n/ac/ax capability

Recommended Operating Systems:
Windows 11
Later Version of MAC OS should also be fine.
Chromebook’s are not recommended.

It is the responsibility of the student to ensure their computer is functioning correctly and that they have full administrator rights. NCI cannot provide support for these personal devices.

Student Laptop Loan Scheme

A Laptop Loan Scheme is available for eligible students to assist them in completing their chosen course.
