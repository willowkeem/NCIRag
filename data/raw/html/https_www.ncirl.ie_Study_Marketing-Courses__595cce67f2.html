Marketing Courses

Develop practical skills to enable you to become an effective marketing professional for the 21st century.
Find a course

Studying Marketing at NCI

Gaining a qualification in marketing can be your passport to a career in a variety of areas, whether you’re interested in digital marketing, brand management, market research, sales or product development.

National College of Ireland's marketing programmes are designed to give graduates the skills they need to succeed in the modern marketing world. Graduating with a degree in marketing means following a career path that is futureproofed, exciting, challenging and dynamic.

Visit the course pages to learn how to apply.

MSc in Marketing

This 1 year, full time programme has been specifically designed with the needs of current and future marketing professionals and managers in mind.

MSc in Marketing - Full-Time

The programme offers students real insight and experience into how to plan and execute marketing campaigns and strategies in actual organisations, enabling our graduates to enter management roles, hitting the ground running.

1 Year | Level 9 | Full-Time | Blended

Undergraduate Degree in Marketing

The BA (Honours) in Marketing Practice will allow you to understand marketing campaigns and the strategies behind them and give you the skills to work in this fast-paced area.

BA (Honours) in Marketing Practice

Learn the latest thinking and techniques, working on real, live industry projects, planning, implementing and adapting current campaigns under the supervision of expert lecturers and marketing professionals.

3 Years | Level 8 | Full-Time | Campus
