Accelerated Business Degrees

Part-time undergraduate business degrees condensed into 3 stages across 2 years.
Find a course

Return to Education with a 2 Year Undergraduate Degree

At National College of Ireland, we understand the demands of a busy lifestyle so we have designed our part-time accelerated business courses in a flexible way to ensure that you can work towards your undergraduate degree while keeping up with multiple commitments such as work and family life.

Both of our accelerated degrees in Business Management and HRM Strategy and Practice are condensed into 3 stages across 2 years. This means that you will complete your degree in just 2 short years, half the time of most part-time undergraduate business degrees. Also, the block delivery nature of these programmes will provide the opportunity for more in-depth development of knowledge as learners will be able to focus on one module at a time.

Visit the course pages below to learn how to apply.

 

 

Benefits of an Accelerated Degree

Our Accelerated Business Degrees offer a flexible, fast-tracked path to a fully accredited qualification, designed to fit around your life and career goals.

Block Teaching

Business Programme

The BA (Honours) in Business Management is aimed at people working in business and management areas or intending to move into them and who want to graduate with an undergraduate business qualification. You will gain in-depth knowledge of a wide range of management and general business issues and learn to appreciate wider factors impacting business decision-making such as the organisation culture, entrepreneurship and the external economic environment.

BA (Hons) in Business Management

Modules on this programme include:

  • Entrepreneurship
  • Marketing
  • Leadership
  • Technology for Business

3 Stages | Level 8 | Part-Time | Blended

Apply Online

Human Resource Management Programme

The BA (Honours) in HRM Strategy and Practice is designed to suit the needs of part-time students requiring a professional CIPD recognised qualification and is aimed at people already working in HRM or intending to move into the area. The programme will give you a comprehensive understanding of the key skills needed by human resource professionals.

BA (Hons) in HRM Strategy and Practice

Modules on this programme include:

  • Employment Law
  • Learning and Development
  • Ethics, Equality and Diversity

3 Stages | Level 8 | Part-Time | Blended
