International Scholarships

International Scholarships

Scholarships for International Students

National College of Ireland has an extensive scholarship and bursary portfolio for international students which aims to reward excellence and promote diversity among our student body.

Scholarship opportunities for international students

 

Please ensure that you read both the specific requirements for each scholarship and the overall Terms & Conditions for NCI scholarships. To secure your scholarship, you are required to pay your fees in full within the published deadlines for each scholarship.

Scholarships Automatically Applied

These scholarships are automatically assessed, therefore there is no requirement to submit a scholarship application.

Masters Academic Merit Scholarships

Masters Academic Merit Scholarships

The following scholarships are available for Masters courses starting in September and January. All students who receive an offer to study a Masters course at NCI are automatically assessed for these scholarships based upon their previous academic performance. Applicants can view the criteria for this scholarship in their Country Page and the Entry Requirements Tab. There is no requirement for you to submit a scholarship application form for these scholarships.

Scholarship Name Amount
Academic Merit Scholarship up to €5,000
   

*Please take note of our change in fees for the January 2026 intake. 

Higher Diploma Scholarship

Higher Diploma Scholarship

The following scholarship is available for the Higher Diploma in Science in Computing and the Higher Diploma in Science in Data Analytics (International) starting in September and January. All students who receive an offer to study the Diploma at NCI are automatically assessed for this scholarship based upon their previous academic performance. There is no requirement for you to submit a scholarship application form for this scholarship.

Scholarship Name Amount
Higher Diploma in Science in Computing €4,000
Higher Diploma in Science in Data Analytics (International) €4,000

Undergraduate Academic Scholarships

Undergraduate Academic Scholarships

The following scholarship is available for Undergraduate courses when joining a full programme from Year 1. All students who receive an offer to study an Undergraduate course at NCI are automatically assessed for these scholarships based upon a range of criteria, including their academic transcripts. There is no requirement for you to submit a scholarship application form for these scholarships.

Scholarship Name Amount
Undergraduate Academic Scholarship (from January 2026 onwards)*  €2,000-€3000 per year

Early Childhood Education and Care (ECEC) Scholarship

Early Childhood Education and Care (ECEC) Scholarship

The following scholarship is available for the BA (Hons) in Early Childhood Education and Care course starting in September. All students who receive an offer to study for the Early Childhood Education and Care undergraduate course at NCI are automatically assessed for this scholarship based on their previous academic performance. There is no requirement for you to submit a scholarship application form for this scholarship.

Scholarship Name Amount
ECEC Scholarship €4,000 (per year)

Scholarships That Require an Application

These Scholarships require a scholarship application.

Young Future Leaders Programme Scholarship

Young Future Leaders Programme Scholarship

The following scholarship is available for International Students when joining an Undergraduate Full-Time programme from Year 1. All students who receive an offer to study an Undergraduate course at NCI and a participation certificate from the Young Future Leaders Programme are eligible to apply for the YFLP Scholarship. 

Students are required to submit a scholarship application form for this scholarship by the deadline 31st March 2025. Applications open from November 2024 for this scholarship for the 2025 academic year. 

Note: This scholarship cannot be used in conjunction with another NCI scholarship.

Scholarship Name Amount
Young Future Leaders Programme Scholarship €3,000 (per year)

Sports Scholarship

Sports Scholarship

The following scholarship is available for full-time international undergraduate registered students who have reached or have the potential to reach a very high level of performance competing at International, National, Inter-provincial or Inter-county level. Details about the scholarship and how to apply are available at NCI's Sports Scholarship. 

The Sports Scholarship can be used in conjunction with another NCI scholarship. The total amount of sporting and academic scholarships a student can receive may be capped in certain cases. Students who receive funding from an external sporting organisation, such as Governing Bodies, can still avail of the NCI Sports scholarship.

Scholarship Name Amount
Sports Scholarship €2,000

Government Funded Scholarships

These Scholarships are funded by the Irish Government and require an application.

Government of Ireland Scholarship

Government of Ireland Scholarship

The Government of Ireland International Education Scholarship is a prestigious scholarship for high-calibre international students. There are 60 places awarded across Irish Higher Education Institutions, with up to 2 scholarships awarded to applicants for National College of Ireland (NCI).

While NCI fund a maximum of two scholarships, it is not guaranteed that NCI applicants will receive a GOI-IES scholarship.  NCI prioritize and shortlist applications as follows:

*high-calibre applicants will have been offered the highest NCI academic scholarship.

  1. *High-calibre applicants with a full offer.
  2. Applicants for any masters programme in the School of Business and Social Sciences.
  3. Applicants with a full offer from the following masters programmes in the School of Computing.
    a.    MSc in Artificial Intelligence
    b.    MSc in Artificial Intelligence for Business
    c.    MSc in Fintech
    d.    MSc in Open Data Practice

    4.

How to apply for the Government of Ireland Scholarship

For information on eligibility, key dates and how to apply please see the Government of Ireland International Education Scholarship website.
