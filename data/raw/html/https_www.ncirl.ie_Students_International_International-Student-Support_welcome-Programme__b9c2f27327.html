Welcome Programme

Starting at NCI

The Welcome Programme is a combination of events designed to ensure new international students have all the information they need to help them settle into college life and get established in Ireland. The programme runs twice a year, one month before classes start in September and January. We encourage students to join as many events as possible to meet new people and settle in.

Welcome Programme Events

We have prepared a mix of in-person and online events delivered to suit you wherever you are in Ireland or arriving shortly. There are three types of events which you can join. The first is a Welcome Meeting scheduled for everyone. The second is highly recommended and, lastly, optional to attend. Learn more about the sessions below:

Welcome Meeting

The Welcome Meeting is an in-person session where you meet with International Peer Mentors, current NCI students or recent graduates, and a small group of freshers like you. You will receive important information, a welcome pack, take a campus tour and ask questions in real-time.

Your Welcome Meeting is scheduled after you complete and send the Registration Form to International Student Support. Up to three days before the meeting, we will send your meeting's time, date and location to your personal email address.

Student Letters

  • Immigration and Welcome letter: The letters will be sent to you within 3 working days of the welcome meeting via your student email.

Highly Recommended Meetings

There are three highly recommended meetings we strongly suggest every student attend:

1. Essentials: at this meeting, you will receive information on registering with immigration, opening a bank account, getting your tax number (PPSN) and working in Ireland.

  • Date & Time: 9th January at 2pm
  • Location: Theatre 3 (Mayor Square campus, third floor)
  • Link to the presentation slides: see link here

2. Student Life: at this meeting, you will receive information on how to succeed in your studies, the support systems available at NCI, and how to adjust to student life.

  • Date & Time: 13th January at 2pm
  • Location: Theatre 3 (Mayor Square campus, third floor)
  • Link to the Presentation slides: see link here

3. Orientation: is organised by Student Services for all new students and is held the week before classes start. Students can expect an email from orientation@ncirl.ie with an invitation to join orientation. Orientation Information is also available on Full-time Registration and Orientation.

Optional Meetings

Optional meetings are events designed to help connect with new students and explore Dublin and Ireland. Join as many events as possible to meet new people and settle in. Optional meetings are:

  • Workshops sessions with practical tips and advice to get to grips with Ireland, for example: Accommodation Workshop, Getting Around, Wellness​, and Safety in Dublin
  • Meet-Up sessions allow you to meet with your international peer mentors and new students like you. It's a great way to break the ice and make friends, for example: Shopping in Dublin, Ice-Breakers, Irish Treats Tasting​, Board Games​
  • Excursions sessions are perfectly designed to help you discover and explore your new home, Dublin, Ireland. We will bring you on exciting adventures to tour around the city, countryside, even other counties. Examples: City Tours, Howth, Dun Laoghaire, and Glendalough

Welcome Programme Schedule: January 2026

The Welcome Programme for the January 2026 intake begins on 5th January and is designed to help you feel confident and supported as you start your journey with NCI.

The event schedule will be updated on this page every Friday, one week before each programme week begins. Students who have confirmed their place will also receive a reminder newsletter by email on the same day.

Reminder: All students need to plan to be in Ireland for classes starting on 19th January 2026.
