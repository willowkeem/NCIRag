NCI Masters Programmes

Masters Programmes for International Students

International students can choose from the following full-time Masters courses at National College of Ireland.

Information on the Masters programmes available for International students at NCI.

NCI School of Business and Social Sciences

  • MSc in Management
  • MSc in International Business
  • MA in Human Resource Management
  • MSc in Finance
  • MSc in Accounting
  • MSc in Marketing
  • MSc in Business Analytics for Decision Makers

NCI School of Computing

  • MSc in Artificial Intelligence
  • MSc in Artificial Intelligence for Business
  • MSc in Data Analytics
  • MSc in Cloud Computing
  • MSc in Cybersecurity
  • MSc in Fintech
  • MSc in Open Data Practice

Looking for an IT programme but don't have an IT bachelor degree? Take a look at NCI's Higher Diploma in Science in Computing
